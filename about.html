About Apex Auto Styling & Mechanics

Learn about our history, our core mission, and the passionate team behind our vehicle modifications and maintenance.

Our History

Established as a small local workshop, Apex Auto Styling & Mechanics was founded on a passion for automotive performance. Over time, we have expanded into a premier destination specializing in mechanical maintenance and specialized aftermarket modifications for utility vehicles and modern bakkies.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission: To provide high-quality, reliable mechanical services and styling upgrades that enhance vehicle performance and aesthetics.

Our Vision: To become the premier local destination for automotive enthusiasts seeking trusted modifications, expert craftsmanship, and reliable servicing.

Our Workshop Team

Head Mechanical Specialist: Oversees engine diagnostics, servicing, and performance tuning.

Custom Styling Lead: Manages body kits, suspension upgrades, and aesthetic customizations.

Service Advisor: Ensures smooth customer queries, booking management, and project consultations.

Why Choose Apex Auto?

Established as a specialized local workshop, we bridge the gap between quality mechanical repairs and high-end vehicle customization.

  • Expert Repairs: Engine servicing & diagnostics.
  • Custom Styling: Body kits & suspension upgrades.
  • Off-Road Specialists: Performance add-ons built tough.
